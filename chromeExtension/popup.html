Navigate with simple mouse gestures:

  • Go Forward: Draw right (R)
  • Go Back: Draw left (L)
  • Close Tab: Draw down-right (DR)
  • Reopen Tab: Draw up-right (UR)

For the most reliable gestures, use a middle or side button.

Right-click gestures can conflict with some sites' menus. If things break, switch to the middle or a side button.

Lower = needs more movement, Higher = reacts to small movement.

Lower = small gestures, Higher = large, deliberate gestures.
